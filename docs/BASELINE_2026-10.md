## 2. Architecture & Routes

| Route | Purpose | Key Components |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Landing page explaining Source Drift, consensus model, and live citation feeds | `Landing.tsx`, `Header.tsx` |
| `/workbench` | Interactive workstation for citation registration, drift audits, claim assertions, and badge verification | `Workbench.tsx`, `TxPipelineModal.tsx`, `Header.tsx` |

### Services and Utilities
- `src/services/contractService.ts`: GenLayer client read/write wrappers (`registerCitation`, `auditCitation`, `publishClaim`, `refreshClaim`, readbacks).
- `src/lib/txLifecycle.ts`: Transaction lifecycle states and terminal status evaluation.
- `src/lib/revertMap.ts`: Decodes 66 distinct contract UserErrors.
- `src/lib/normalization.ts`: Text normalizers mirroring contract logic.
- `src/lib/outcome.ts`: Evaluates drift verdict outcomes and badge colors.
